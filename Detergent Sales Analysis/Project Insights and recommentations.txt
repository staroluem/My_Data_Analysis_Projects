# Sales-Analysis-Project

🧼 Detergent Sales Analysis & Forecasting Web App
During my internship, I developed a Detergent Sales Analysis & Forecasting System aimed at diagnosing the reasons behind an 11% year-over-year decline in detergent sales for September. I integrated multiple datasets—including transaction, product, and store-level data—and performed extensive data cleaning and preprocessing using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Jupyter Notebook. I analyzed sales patterns across customer segments, regions, time periods, and promotional activities, and built Power BI dashboards to visualize key trends such as engagement levels, price sensitivity, discount impact, and regional performance. To deepen the analysis, I developed an XGBoost regression model to predict sales and applied SHAP explainability and feature importance analysis to identify the most impactful factors contributing to the decline. Insights revealed reduced promotional discounts, fewer high-traffic days such as weekends, increased price-sensitive customers, declining customer engagement, and significantly lower transactions in regions like Lombardia. Based on these findings, I proposed business recommendations such as increasing promotional offers for price-sensitive customers, improving competitive pricing across local markets, enhancing engagement in underperforming stores, and leveraging influencer-driven marketing to boost sales. The project was delivered as a web-based analytical application integrating dataset views, visual insights, and forecasting tools, helping stakeholders optimize inventory, promotions, and strategy.

Insights taken out from data why 11% decrease in detergent sales:

1.Decrease in the discount on products.

2.One Saturday was less in sep2019 which individually decremented sales by 9%.

3.Increase in Price sensitive as well as Price neutral customers which is one of the possible reason for decrease in Detergent sale.

4.The chart shows that Lombardia had a significant drop in the number of transactions in 2019. This could be a key factor in the decrease in sales.

5.Decrease in the customer engagement with retailer, as Elite(1), Good(2) and Occasional(3) engagement decreased and Unassigned increased.
